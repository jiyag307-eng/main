Why TrainSync?

Problem Context
Multi-Department Block Coordination

A single railway section may require maintenance from multiple departments at the same time — Engineering, TRD (Traction Distribution), and S&T (Signal & Telecommunication).

Currently, these maintenance activities can be planned independently, which may result in the same railway section being blocked multiple times (multiple separate blocks). This can increase track downtime, reduce block utilization efficiency, and potentially disrupt train operations.

TrainSync coordinates compatible maintenance activities across departments and identifies opportunities to combine them into one combined optimized block.
